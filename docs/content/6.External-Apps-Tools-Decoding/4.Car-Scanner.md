# Car Scanner ELM OBD2 Setup

**Car Scanner ELM OBD2** is one of the most popular and versatile diagnostic apps available for iOS and Android. It is an excellent tool for reading and clearing Check Engine Lights (DTCs), viewing live sensor data, and checking emissions readiness.

The WiCAN Pro acts as a high-speed wireless ELM327 adapter, making it fully compatible with the Car Scanner app via Wi-Fi or Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).

---

## Phase 1: WiCAN Pro Configuration

Before opening the app on your phone, you must ensure the WiCAN Pro is broadcasting the correct protocol for third-party OBD2 apps.

1. Access the WiCAN Pro Web UI.
2. Navigate to **Settings > CAN**.
3. Ensure the **Protocol** dropdown is set to `elm327`.
4. Check your connection details below that:
   * **Port Type:** Set to `TCP`
   * **TCP/UDP Port:** Note this number (typically `3333` by default).
5. Ensure your phone is connected to the WiCAN Pro's network. 
   * If you are in the car, connect your phone directly to the WiCAN's Wi-Fi hotspot (AP Mode).
   * If you are at home, ensure both your phone and the WiCAN are on the same home Wi-Fi network (Station Mode).

> [!NOTE]
> If you prefer to use Bluetooth instead of Wi-Fi, ensure **BLE Status** is enabled in the **Settings > BLE** tab. Note that when a BLE connection is active, the WiCAN's Wi-Fi Access Point is temporarily disabled.

---

## Phase 2: App Setup

1. Download and open **Car Scanner ELM OBD2** on your iOS or Android device.
2. Tap the **Settings** (gear) icon in the top or bottom corner of the screen.
3. Tap on **Connection**.

### For Wi-Fi Connections (Recommended for Speed)
* **Connection Type:** Select `Wi-Fi`.
* **IP Address:** Enter the WiCAN's IP address.
  * If connected directly to the WiCAN's hotspot (AP Mode), this is usually `192.168.0.10`.
  * If connected through your home network (Station Mode), enter the IP assigned to the WiCAN by your router.
* **Port:** Enter `3333` (or the specific port you verified in Phase 1).

### For Bluetooth (BLE) Connections
* **Connection Type:** Select `Bluetooth LE (4.0+)`.
* **Device Name:** Tap "Select Device" and choose your WiCAN Pro from the list of available Bluetooth devices.

---

## Phase 3: Connecting to Your Vehicle

1. Ensure your vehicle's ignition is turned **ON** (engine running is preferred for live data).
2. Return to the main dashboard of the Car Scanner app.
3. Tap the large **Connect** button at the bottom of the screen.
4. If this is your first time connecting, the app will prompt you to select your vehicle make and model profile. Choose the one that matches your vehicle to ensure custom sensors load correctly.
5. The app will run through an initialization sequence. The connection icons at the top of the screen (Phone to ELM, ELM to ECU) should turn green.

You are now ready to read trouble codes, view live dashboards, and run diagnostic tests!

> [!WARNING]
> **Remember to Disconnect:** The Car Scanner app actively polls the CAN bus for data. If you leave it connected and running while trying to configure the WiCAN's Automate tab or other features in the Web UI, you may experience data collisions. Always tap "Disconnect" in the app when you are finished using it.
