# Standard OBD2 PIDs

The **Standard PIDs** tab is the easiest way to start pulling data from your vehicle. These are universal parameters that the WiCAN Pro can request without any custom coding.

---

## The Configuration Process

Follow these steps to start monitoring standard vehicle data:

### Step 1: Access the Menu
1. Connect to your WiCAN Web Interface.
2. Navigate to **Automate** > **Automate Parameters**.
3. Click on the **Standard PIDs** tab.

### Step 2: Scan and Add Available PIDs
Unlike custom PIDs, the WiCAN can "ask" your car which standard parameters it supports.
1. **Select ECU Protocol:** Choose the protocol matching your vehicle (e.g., ISO 15765-4 CAN 11bit 500kbps).
2. **Press Scan:** Click the **Scan** button to ping the vehicle's ECU.
   > [!IMPORTANT]
   > Most vehicles require the **engine to be running** (or at least the ignition in the "ON" position) for the ECU to respond to a PID scan.
3. **Select from List:** Once the scan is complete, click the **Available PIDs:** dropdown menu. This will now be populated with every standard PID your car officially supports.
4. **Add to List:** Select a PID from the dropdown and click **Add**. It will appear in the table below.
5. **Repeat:** Continue selecting and adding all the parameters you wish to monitor.

### Step 3: Enable and Set Frequency
Once your PIDs are in the table:
1. Toggle the **Enable** switch to **ON** for the PIDs you want active.
2. In the **Interval (ms)** field, enter how often the WiCAN should poll this data.
   * **High Speed (100ms):** Best for RPM or Throttle.
   * **Medium Speed (1000ms):** Good for Vehicle Speed.

### Step 4: Routing and Logging
1. **Destination:** Choose which **User Destination** (Server) should receive this data. 
2. **Log to SD:** Check this box if you want this specific PID recorded to the local microSD card.

### Step 5: Save Configuration
1. Scroll to the bottom and click **Save Configuration**.

### Step 6: Organizing with Vehicle Groups
If you are using **Vehicle Groups** to manage different cars:
1. Navigate to the **Vehicle Groups** tab.
2. Select or create your group.
3. Click the **"Copy Standard PIDs"** button to instantly import your enabled list into that group.

---

## Performance Tip
Only add the PIDs you actually intend to use. While the scan might find 40+ available PIDs, polling all of them at high speeds can saturate the CAN bus and slow down your critical data.

| Parameter | Recommended Interval |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Engine RPM** | 100 ms |
| **Coolant Temp** | 10,000 ms |
| **Fuel Level** | 30,000 ms |
