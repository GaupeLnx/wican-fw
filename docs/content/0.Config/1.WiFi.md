## Access Point Mode (Initial Setup)

By default, your WiCAN will act as an Access Point and create its own WiFi network named **`WiCAN_xxxxxxx`** (where `xxxxxxx` is the unique device ID of the WiCAN you are using). You can connect to it with the default password `@meatpi#`. 

*If you are using a phone, you may need to wait a few seconds and confirm you want to connect to a network that "does not provide internet."*

Once connected, open `http://192.168.0.10` in your browser to access the user interface of the WiCAN. Note, you must disable any VPN on your phone, before attempting to connect.

![image](/config/wifi/accesspoint.png)

It is highly recommended that you change the Access point password in the user interface on the "Settings" tab. Otherwise, anyone can connect to your WiCAN if they get close to your car while the WiCAN is powered on.

**Note: If you intend to use the device strictly in AP mode, it is recommended that you disable the BLE function.**

## 1. AP Configuration (Advanced)
The Wi-Fi Settings tab is where you dictate how your WiCAN Pro communicates with your devices and your local network. This advanced firmware fork introduces major enhancements.

* **Mode:** Select your primary operating mode:
    * **AP Mode:** The device solely broadcasts its own network.
    * **AP+Station:** Broadcasts its own network *while* connecting to your home/garage Wi-Fi.
    * **BLE+Station:** Disables the Wi-Fi Access point entirely, using Bluetooth Low Energy and your local Wi-Fi router. 
    * **Station Mode:** Connects strictly to an existing Wi-Fi router. 
    * *(Note: If you lock yourself out by disabling the AP, press and hold the physical button on the device for 5 seconds to reset the AP).*
* **AP Channel:** Select the Wi-Fi broadcast channel (1-14). Change this if you are experiencing interference.
* **AP SSID [NEW]:** You can now customize the name of the network the WiCAN broadcasts! Check the box and enter a name (3 to 32 characters). Unchecking it reverts to the default unique hardware SSID.
* **Auto-Disable AP [NEW]:** Highly recommended if using **AP+Station** mode. It automatically turns off the WiCAN's Access Point once a successful connection to your local router is established, preventing IP subnet conflicts and routing drops.

## 2. Network Configuration (Station Mode)
If you want the WiCAN to join your home network, mobile hotspot, or garage Wi-Fi, configure it here.

* **MAC Address:** Displays your device's unique hardware identifier (useful for setting up DHCP reservations on your router).
* **SSID & Wi-Fi Scanner [NEW]:** You no longer have to blindly type your network name! Click the **Scan** button. The WiCAN will search for surrounding networks and display them in a dropdown, complete with Signal Quality (Excellent/Good/Fair/Weak), exact RSSI (dBm), and security type.
* **Password:** Enter the password for your local network.
* **IP Assignment [NEW]:** * **DHCP:** Automatically grabs an IP address from your router.
    * **Static IP:** Manually assign a Static IP, Subnet Mask, Gateway, and DNS Server.

## 3. Backup Networks / Fallbacks [NEW]
Never lose connection to your WiCAN on the road again. You can now configure up to **5 fallback networks**.

* Click **Add SSID** to add a backup network.
* If the primary Station network drops or is out of range, the WiCAN will sequentially attempt to connect to these backup networks.
* *Pro Tip:* Great for adding your phone's mobile hotspot as a backup for when you drive away from your home router. You can even set independent **Static IP or DHCP** rules for *each* fallback network!

## 4. SmartConnect Configuration [NEW]
*SmartConnect* is an advanced routing profile designed for users who want the WiCAN to behave differently parked in the garage versus driving on the road.

**Home Mode:**
Triggered when your Home SSID is in range. 
* Define your Home Wi-Fi credentials and select a protocol (e.g., `elm327` or `AutoPID`). 

**Drive Mode:**
Triggered when you leave the range of your Home SSID.
* **Drive Connection Type:** Choose to seamlessly fall back to **WiFi** (like a mobile hotspot) or **BLE** (Bluetooth Low Energy) for your dashboard app.
* **Drive Mode Timeout:** Adjust the slider (10 to 600 seconds) to dictate how long the device should search before transitioning modes.
